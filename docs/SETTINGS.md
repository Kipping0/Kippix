# Settings reference

Open settings with the **⋯** icon in Kippix's top bar (*Settings & Customisation*). This page follows the menu from top to
bottom.

A few things that apply everywhere:

- **Search settings** sits at the top of the menu: type part of a setting's name to jump straight to it.
- Settings only appear when they apply. For example, trailer options show once trailers are switched on, and each layout's
  options show only while you're using that layout.
- Every page ends with **Reset this page**. Bigger resets are in [Layouts & reset](#layouts--reset).
- Pages about the game page open a game's page behind the settings, so you see each change as you make it.

## Look and screens

### Home settings

How the home screen looks and behaves.

| Page | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Game cards and layout** | *Games shown as*: Row (Lower, Tall or Full Screen), Grid, List, Board, Manual board, Folders (work in progress). Presets for the layout, card size, shape and spacing, and options for the chosen layout (for example looping round in Row). |
| **Logo, game info & button hints** | The highlighted game's logo, which info lines show (play time, last played, players now, friends playing, story progress…), the button hints and the current filter name, and where each sits. |
| **Tile badges** | Small badges on game tiles (installed, update waiting, on sale and so on). |
| **Games kept in place** | Games pinned to a fixed spot in the row. |
| **Trailer preview** | A game's trailer after a short wait: in the card (which widens) or as the background, with or without sound. |
| **Widgets** | Add, change and remove widgets: weather, clock and date, game suggestions, spotlight, free games this week, wishlist deals, wishlist releases, downloads, friends online, PC stats, latest capture, text and picture tiles. |
| **Background** | Where it comes from (game art, plain colour, trailer preview, your own picture, muted trailer, slideshow, the game's tile, or BackgroundChanger's), fade to black, moving the picture, change speed, zoom and depth. |
| **Tiles before your games** | Library, Store, Achievements and Up next tiles: hidden, shown when you move left past the first game, or always. |
| **Extra rows** | Favourites, Recently added, Installed, Never played, Up next, your shelves, Playnite saved filters. |
| **Seasonal effects / Weather effects** | Seasonal effects, and weather effects following your real weather or a schedule. |
| **Pressing A on a game** | Open its game page, or play it straight away. |
| **Free move** | Move, resize, rotate or hide anything on the home screen (or hold A on it). Snap to a grid, and the grid size. |
| **Put things back** | Undo moves one part at a time. |

### Game page

The page you get when you press A on a game.

| Page | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Layout** | Default (logo and buttons left, art right) or alternative (art on top, info centred); the logo, info and description; Free move for the page. |
| **Game media** | Cover, trailer or nothing; trailer autoplay, sound, loop and the buttons under it. |
| **Play info** | Which lines show, such as play time, last played, size, *How long to beat*. |
| **Buttons & extras** | The buttons next to Play, achievements, the clock, the DLC note. |
| **Top and lower page** | Every part of the page, which page it's on and its settings. The lower page's cards: achievements, story progress, *How long to beat*, play history, notes, scores, status, save backups, mods, news, your captures, more like this in your library. |
| **Friends** | Which friends play this game (needs PlayerActivities, and optionally a Steam Web API key). |
| **Animation** | How the page comes in: Off (straight away), Rise, Fade, From the sides, Drop in, Zoom in, Settle, Cinematic, Cascade, Pop or Focus. Picking one plays it on the page behind. |
| **Background** | A slow zoom on the game's art, or a black background. |

### Top bar

Your shortcuts (pin, unpin and reorder: Store, Achievements, Gallery, Search, Collections, Downloads, Sound output,
Bluetooth, Quick settings, Hibernate, Sleep, Restart, Power off…), the notifications icon, controller battery, the clock (12 or 24 hours), and
**Auto-hide**: the bar fades away until you move up to it.

### Store settings

Which stores show on the Store page, which shops prices come from (official stores, plus resellers if you want), your
currency or country for prices, wishlist sync, where store pages open (Kippix's browser or the store's app), trailers on
store pages and the browser's cursor speed.

## Everywhere

### Audio

- **Menu sounds**: on or off, style and volume.
- **Music**: off, Playnite's music, your own music folder (shuffled), or per-game music with UniPlaySong. Music fades
  out while a game runs, while Playnite is in the background and while a trailer plays with sound.

### General settings

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Look and feel** | Look packs, Theme Studio, accent colour, button icons (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, keyboard, or automatic), labels on icons, night mode (off, automatic 22:00–07:00, always), screensaver, intro and outro (none, or your own video). |
| **Language and add-ons** | Kippix follows Playnite's language (only English is included so far). **Other add-ons** lists the add-ons Kippix works with that you have installed. |
| **Controller** | *Hold A to move*, the Guide button (goes home, or opens the quick bar), what B does on the home screen (first game, top bar, nothing), **Controller buttons** (move Kippix's actions to other buttons, per screen), **Handheld, touch and mouse** (handheld mode, touch gestures, where the mouse pointer goes). |
| **Steam** | Set new Steam games to update straight away (high priority). The same is in the profile menu as a one-off. |
| **In-game overlay** | Switch the overlay on or off. Its other settings are in the overlay and in desktop mode, see [In-game overlay](OVERLAY.md#settings). |

### Notifications

The card shown after you finish playing (with your session's captures), the play-time reminder, Steam and wishlist notices
(sales, releases), game update notices, and the notification card's style and position.

## Your setup

### Layouts & reset

- **Your layouts**: save the current layout under a name, switch between saved ones, upload one you were sent (with the
  controller's file picker), save a copy to a folder.
- **Reset**: the home screen, the game page, or everything. Your wishlist, keys and play history are kept, and the last
  reset can be undone.

Saved layouts are in `Documents\Kippix Layouts`.

### Accessibility

Bigger text, less motion (reduce motion: pages show straight away, nothing slides or zooms) and high contrast.

### Profiles

A preview feature, switched on in desktop mode (Add-ons › Kippix Helper › Testing). Who's playing at start-up, a layout
and settings per person, what each profile can open, age ratings, daily time limits and the grown-ups' PIN. See
[the guide](GUIDE.md#10-profiles-and-time-limits).

## Community

Get layouts, game page designs, look packs and widgets other people have shared, and share your own. Pressing **Share**
saves the files in `Documents\Kippix Layouts\Share` and opens the sharing form on GitHub. Every shared item is checked
before it goes into the gallery. Community widgets that fetch things from the web (web snippets, news feeds, data widgets)
only work after you switch them on.

## Help

- **Check for updates**
- **GitHub** (this page)
- **Kippix Doctor**: health checks (see [Troubleshooting](GUIDE.md#12-troubleshooting))
- **Take the tour again**
- **Tips**: switch first-time tips off or show them again
- **Save a log**: a bug report for support, saved in `Documents\Kippix Logs`
- **Detailed logging**: more detail in the logs while you track down a problem

## Kippix Helper in Playnite's desktop mode

Playnite desktop mode › Add-ons › **Kippix Helper**:

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Kippix** | Friends on the game page and the optional Steam Web API key; layout resets and undo; *Forgotten your PIN?*; **Testing**: profiles, the tour at every start, and where the Community gallery comes from. |
| **In-game overlay** | Opening buttons, what happens to the game, screenshots and clips, pop-ups, Discord and the rest. |
| **Achievement guides** | The AI service and your key (optional). |
| **Doctor** | Health checks, safe mode, *Offer safe mode after failed starts*, and *Make a bug report*. |
